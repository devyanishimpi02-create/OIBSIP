Our story

Started in a home kitchen, one birthday cake at a time.

Honeycomb began when our founder started baking birthday cakes for neighbors because the grocery store version never tasted like anything. Word spread, orders grew, and eventually the home kitchen turned into a proper studio.

We still bake in small batches, from scratch, using real butter and real fruit — never a mix, never a shortcut, no matter how big the order gets.

How custom ordering works

From idea to pickup in four steps

Most custom cakes are ready within 48 hours. Wedding cakes need a bit more lead time for the tasting.

01

Tell us the occasion

Send a quick message with the date, size, and any flavor or design ideas you already have.

02

03
